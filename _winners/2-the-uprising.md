summary: >-
  Broughton is a strict Puritan settlement in colonial Vermont, where Reverend
  James Bradshaw keeps the children in line through harsh punishment and
  oversees the trials and executions of those he judges sinners. One winter
  night he hides in the cellar of his parsonage as something closes in. It is
  a mass of the children he wronged, among them a girl he condemned to hang a
  week before. After they take their revenge, Broughton is wild and free again.
