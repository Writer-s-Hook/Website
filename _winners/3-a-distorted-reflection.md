summary: >-
  A girl with an eating disorder sees only flaws in her bedroom mirror, and
  out of fear of gaining weight she restricts food and even water. When she
  collapses, she wakes in a hospital, treated for low blood sugar and
  dehydration. Back home after a week and a half, she sees in the mirror how
  emaciated she has become, and realizes the distortion was never in the glass
  but in how she saw herself. She decides to start anew.
