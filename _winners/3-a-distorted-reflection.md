Flaccid rolls of fat hang limp around the girl’s midsection. In her dimly lit bedroom, the large, floor-to-ceiling mirror reflects every imperfection in perfect clarity: the big red pimples protruding from her face, the way her tiny muscles hang limp on her body, her too-thick waist—the list could continue forever.

To the girl, her deformed reflection is terrifying. However, despite her overwhelming fear, she cannot help but stare at the mirror. She is captivated by her image, despite how grotesque it appears to her.

After a short while, the girl trudges to the bathroom. She slowly removes her clothes before hesitantly stepping onto the scale.

The number dismays her. Weighing in at half a pound heavier than the previous night, the girl starts to hyperventilate. She quickly jumps off the scale and puts on her clothes, trying her best to avert her eyes from the glowing blue light of the scale.

As the girl heads to the kitchen, her head throbs painfully from the never-ending headache that has plagued her for weeks. Her stomach lets out a whine, but she has grown used to the dull ache coming from her abdomen. The girl opens the refrigerator and reaches for an apple, but quickly draws back her hand. The sugar in an apple could lead her to gain another pound.

She pours a glass of water, but leaves it untouched. She has a complicated relationship with water. Out of fear that it will increase the number on the scale, she tries to restrict her consumption of it as much as possible. Even though her dry throat begs otherwise, she has survived so far on barely any food or drink, and nothing says she can’t continue to live this way.

Yet again, the girl finds herself in front of her bedroom mirror. After her short trip to the kitchen, she has returned with nothing in her stomach. Even though she knows that nothing has changed since her previous encounter with the mirror, the girl still examines every inch of her body. She is certain that her stomach extends just a tiny bit more now than before.

While examining her appearance, a sudden spurt of pain lances through her head. She staggers, grabbing onto the closest thing to her: the door. Dark spots start to swim through her vision, and the girl opens her eyes wider and wider and wider. Her eyeballs feel as if they could pop right out of their sockets, but the gaps in her vision only continue to multiply. Her heart pounds in her chest, expanding into a deafening roar. The girl turns toward the mirror, trying to see her disheveled appearance, and her hands grasp the smooth surface of the mirror as if she were able to see herself through touch. Finally, her vision blacks out, and she loses consciousness before falling to the floor.

When the girl awakes, she is met by the blinding white lights of a hospital—a drastic change compared to the dark room that she normally resides in. Machines beep all around her, and an IV drip is connected to her arm. After a short while, a nurse comes to check up on her. The words that the nurse says seem to pass through the girl. Low blood sugar? Dehydration? What does that mean?

A week and a half later, the girl is discharged from the hospital to head home. She enters her house in a daze. Before long, she finds herself standing in front of the mirror. Her reflection appears different than before. The pimples that used to jut out of her face are now nothing more than tiny red dots that are barely visible. Her midsection, which used to have rolls of pudge, now seems flat as a board. Her ribs are distinct, with each one being visible, and her chest bones show through her skin. She looks emaciated.

The girl does not react for a long time. She stares at her image, unblinking. Her headache is gone, and her stomach no longer throbs. She is the most lucid that she has been in a very long time. Her vision is perfect, leading her to question why her appearance is so drastically different from before. Was it the hospital? Did they do something to her? Deep down, however, she knows the truth. Her eyesight had nothing to do with her distorted appearance—it was herself.

With this new insight fresh in her mind, the girl sinks down onto her bed, ready to start anew.
