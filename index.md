  In a full day event, teens across the Bay Area attend intensive writing workshops,
  a presentation from a published author, and gain inside advice about writing
  program applications and literary magazines.

Writer's Hook is an intensive, inspiring day dedicated entirely to the craft of writing — a day to draft alongside fellow high school writers, sit with authors and editors who take your work seriously, and leave with something that did not exist that morning.

Whatever you write, and however far along you are, there is a table here for you.
